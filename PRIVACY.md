# Privacy Policy — Print Shop Job Manager

Effective: September 19, 2026

Print Shop Job Manager is published by Worksbien Studios.

The app is local-first. Job records, production status, import previews, history, and backups are processed on the device. Worksbien Studios does not operate an account system or cloud service for this version of the app, and the app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking.

The app accesses files only when a user selects a CSV or backup through the system file picker, or chooses to export a CSV or backup. Exported files go only to the destination selected by the user. Users are responsible for protecting files they export or share.

Apple processes subscription purchases and related transaction information under Apple's privacy terms. Worksbien Studios receives only the StoreKit entitlement information needed to unlock Pro; the app does not receive full payment-card details.

Deleting the app removes its on-device workspace unless a separate device backup or user-exported backup retains it. Users can remove sample content inside Settings.

For privacy questions, contact support@worksbienstudios.com.
